        Reading the chart

        The curved lines are the World Health Organization growth standards for boys, the charts used in Australia from birth to 2 years. On the 50th percentile line, half of boys the same age weigh less and half weigh more. On the 85th, 85 in 100 weigh less.

        A baby does not need to be on any particular line. The usual pattern is a path that runs roughly alongside one of the curves over time. Babies often lose some weight in the first few days and are back to birth weight by about 2 weeks.

        General information only. Your maternal and child health nurse or GP is the right person to ask about Grayson's growth. Percentiles are worked out from the WHO weight-for-age standards.
